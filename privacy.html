A PUZZLE SERIES · PRIVACY POLICY

The short version

Ensemble collects no data. None. Not anonymized, not aggregated, not "only for analytics." Nothing leaves your device.

The complete version

Ensemble has no accounts, no sign-in, no analytics, no advertising, no tracking SDKs, and no third-party code of any kind. The app makes no network requests. Your progress — stars, streaks, awards, settings — is stored only on your device and is never transmitted anywhere.

The Daily Table Read puzzle is generated on your device from the date; no server is involved. The optional daily reminder is a local notification, scheduled and delivered entirely by iOS on your device — enabling it shares nothing with us or anyone else.

Because no data is collected, there is nothing for us to sell, share, lose, or be subpoenaed for. Ensemble's App Store privacy label reads "Data Not Collected," and this page is the whole policy.

Changes

If a future update ever changes any of the above, this policy will be updated before that version ships, with the change plainly described.
