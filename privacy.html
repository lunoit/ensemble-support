A PUZZLE SERIES · PRIVACY POLICY

The short version

We collect no data. None. Not anonymized, not aggregated, not "only for analytics." The only things that ever leave your device go to your own Apple account — iCloud sync and, if you sign in, Game Center — and none of it passes through us.

The complete version

Ensemble has no Luno accounts, no analytics, no advertising, no tracking SDKs, and no third-party code of any kind. We run no servers, so there is nothing of ours for the app to talk to. Your progress — stars, streaks, awards, settings — lives on your device.

The Daily Table Read puzzle is generated on your device from the date; no server is involved. The optional daily reminder is a local notification, scheduled and delivered entirely on your device — enabling it shares nothing with us or anyone else.

iCloud

If your device is signed into iCloud, your progress syncs between your own devices — phone, tablet, television — through your Apple Account. That data (the same stars, streaks and awards) is stored in your personal iCloud and handled by Apple under Apple's privacy policy. We cannot read it, and it never touches a Luno server. Without iCloud the game simply stays local.

Game Center

Game Center is optional and entirely Apple's. If you accept the sign-in, your achievements and leaderboard entries — total stars, daily streak, and your daily puzzle score — are reported to Game Center under your Game Center profile, governed by Apple's privacy policy; leaderboards show them to the friends you chose there. Decline the sheet, once, and the game is exactly the same game, fully local. Either way, nothing reaches us.

Because nothing reaches us, there is nothing for us to sell, share, lose, or be subpoenaed for. Ensemble's App Store privacy label reads "Data Not Collected," and this page is the whole policy.

Changes

If a future update ever changes any of the above, this policy will be updated before that version ships, with the change plainly described.
