Frequently asked

How do I play?
Swipe anywhere on the board. Every critter moves in that direction at once, and each must reach the exit of its own color. That's the whole show — the drama is in the order.
I'm stuck.
The HINT button offers three cue cards per attempt, computed from your exact position. If no sequence of moves can win anymore, the game calls "CUT!" on its own and offers an undo or retake.
When does the Daily Table Read reset?
At midnight, your local time. Everyone on Earth gets the same puzzle each day.
How do I back up my progress?
Progress lives only on your device — there are no accounts and no cloud. It survives app updates, but deleting the app deletes your stars. CLIFF suggests not deleting the app.
Does Ensemble collect my data?
No. Nothing is collected, tracked, or sent anywhere. See the privacy policy — it's refreshingly short.
